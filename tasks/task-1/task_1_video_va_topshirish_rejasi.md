### A. Ingliz tilida (Tavsiya etilgan)
* **Project Name:**
  `ShifoHalqa — Smart Home Care & Rehab Verification Loop`
* **Short Description:**
  `ShifoHalqa turns a doctor’s post-visit plan (medication, sit-to-stand rehab exercises, blood pressure, and activity targets) into verifiable daily tasks in a mobile web browser. Using in-browser pose estimation, vision-based medication/vitals reading, and deterministic clinical rules, it filters daily logs so doctors only review patients who need attention.`
* **Problem Statement:**
  `In Fergana and across Uzbekistan, patients managing chronic hypertension or recovering after joint/orthopedic issues spend a brief consultation in the clinic, followed by weeks of unsupervised treatment at home. During this gap, three recurring failures occur: (1) patients can confuse medication dosages when purchasing new boxes (e.g., 5 mg vs 10 mg), (2) home rehabilitation exercises are often skipped or performed improperly without guidance, and (3) warning signs like rising blood pressure or pain reach the doctor too late. Meanwhile, polyclinic doctors do not have time to check on dozens of patients manually every day, and current reminders or electronic records do not track verified home adherence.`
* **Proposed Solution:**
  `ShifoHalqa connects doctors, patients, and self-care users through a browser-based "Verification Ladder" MVP:`
  1. `Template-Based Care Plans: Doctors assign structured medication, blood pressure, step, and sit-to-stand exercise targets in seconds (while individual users can also track personal wellness tasks).`
  2. `Medication Tracking & "DoriMos" Vision Check: Patients log doses and can photograph a medication box label; Vision AI extracts the drug name and strength for patient confirmation, and code rules immediately flag any dosage mismatch against the prescription.`
  3. `Privacy-First Browser Exercise Engine: Using in-browser MediaPipe Pose (via live camera or uploaded test video), the app counts sit-to-stand reps and 30-second chair stand tests, measuring knee angles and a confidence score without sending raw video to the server by default. Basic anti-replay checks include daily countable limits, SHA-256 file hashing, and motion signatures.`
  4. `Exception-Based Doctor Loop: Deterministic rules (not AI diagnosis) raise yellow/red flags for high blood pressure, medication mismatches, pain reports, or low-confidence logs. With explicit patient consent, short clips or photos enter the doctor's review queue for verification.`
* **Planned Technologies:**
  `Frontend: Next.js (React), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (in-browser WebAssembly/WebGL), Web Crypto API (SHA-256). Backend & Data: Next.js API Routes, PostgreSQL / SQLite, Deterministic JSON Rules Engine. AI Layer: Multimodal Vision/LLM API strictly for OCR extraction (medication boxes, blood pressure monitors) and concise doctor log summaries.`

### B. O‘zbek tilida (Muqobil variant)
* **Project Name:**
  `ShifoHalqa — Uyda davolanish va reabilitatsiyani aqlli nazorat qilish tizimi`
* **Short Description:**
  `ShifoHalqa shifokor yozgan uy davolanish rejasini (dori-darmon, reabilitatsiya mashqlari, qon bosimi va qadamlar) oddiy telefon brauzerida bajariladigan vazifalarga aylantiradi. Brauzerda ishlovchi poza tahlili, dori qutisini o‘quvchi Vision AI va qat’iy kod qoidalari yordamida shifokorga faqat e’tibor talab qiladigan bemorlarni saralab ko‘rsatadi.`
* **Problem Statement:**
  `Farg‘ona va O‘zbekistonning boshqa hududlarida surunkali gipertoniya bilan og‘rigan yoki bo‘g‘im reabilitatsiyasidan o‘tayotgan bemorlar shifokor qabulida qisqa vaqt bo‘ladi, lekin uyda haftalab nazoratsiz davolanadi. Bu davrda uchta asosiy muammo uchraydi: (1) bemorlar yangi dori qutisi olganda dozasini (masalan, 5 mg va 10 mg) adashtirishi mumkin, (2) uyda buyurilgan mashqlar og‘riqdan qo‘rqib tashlab ketiladi yoki noto‘g‘ri bajariladi, (3) qon bosimi oshishi yoki og‘riq haqida shifokorga kech xabar boradi. Poliklinika shifokorlarida esa har kuni o‘nlab bemorlarga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishga vaqt yetmaydi.`
* **Proposed Solution:**
  `ShifoHalqa "Dalil narvoni" tamoyili asosida shifokor, bemor va mustaqil foydalanuvchilarni bog‘laydi:`
  1. `Tezkor Reja Tuzish: Shifokor tayyor shablonlardan dori, bosim, qadam va o‘tirib-turish mashqlarini biriktiradi.`
  2. `Dori Nazorati va DoriMos: Bemor dorini qayd etadi va dori qutisini suratga oladi; Vision AI nom va dozani o‘qiydi, bemor tasdiqlaydi va kod qoidalari shifokor retsepti bilan solishtirib xatolikni tutadi.`
  3. `Maxfiylikka Asoslangan Kamera Mashq Motori: Brauzerda MediaPipe Pose orqali (jonli kamera yoki yuklangan sinov videosida) o‘tirib-turish takrorlari, tizza burchagi va ishonch bali hisoblanadi; video standart holatda qurilmadan chiqmaydi. Takroriy video yuklashga qarshi kunlik limit, SHA-256 xesh va harakat imzosi tekshiriladi.`
  4. `Istisnolar Paneli va Shifokor Halqasi: Qoidalar dvigateli doza farqi, yuqori bosim, og‘riq yoki past ishonchli mashqni ajratib, bemor roziligi bilan shifokor ko‘rib chiqish navbatiga yuboradi.`
* **Planned Technologies:**
  `Next.js (React, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (WebAssembly), Web Crypto API (SHA-256), PostgreSQL / SQLite, Deterministik JSON Qoidalar Dvigateli, Multimodal Vision/LLM API (dori qutisi va tonometr OCR hamda shifokor uchun qisqa xulosa).`
